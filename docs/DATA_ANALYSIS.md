# Data analysis (before the training sets were built)

Date: 2026-11-04 · Source numbers: [`data_stats.json`](data_stats.json) (generated by `scripts/analyze_data.py`).
No toxic text or lexicon entries are reproduced in this document.

> This is a snapshot from before the training sets were built, kept as a record of what we found in the raw data. Some
> gaps it lists were closed later: access to SurakshaEval and MILU was granted and both are in the final test sets, and
> 40 tricky-benign seeds were written (`data/tricky_seeds.json`). The sets as built are described in
> [PIPELINE.md](PIPELINE.md) and [DATASETS.md](DATASETS.md).

## TL;DR

| # | Finding | Severity | What we did |
|---|---------|----------|--------------------|
| 1 | IndicAlign `hin_Latn` ("romanised Hindi") is machine transliteration, not real Hinglish: 0 × "hai", 31,385 × "haye" in Dolly-T | **High** | Do not use `hin_Latn` for SFT targets. Create natural Hinglish with an LLM rewrite step and have a human spot-check it |
| 2 | All 200 Aya-Eval `dolly_human_edited` Hindi prompts are also in IndicAlign Dolly-T | **High (test leakage)** | Drop those 200 Dolly rows from Set A (match on the English instruction via `databricks-dolly-15k`) |
| 3 | Aya Dataset has only **1,153** Hindi rows (~950 usable), not 10–20K | Medium | Set A must come mostly from IndicAlign Dolly-T + OASST-T (Devanagari) |
| 4 | HH-RLHF-T: **60%** of its 32,669 prompts are exact duplicates (~13K unique), and many first turns are not actually harmful | **High (label noise)** | Dedup, then keep only prompts that Llama Guard flags as unsafe before tagging them `harmful` |
| 5 | No natural Hinglish safety test set: Aya Red-teaming and IndicSafe Hindi are 100% Devanagari | Medium | Build a held-out Hinglish safety split (~200) and report it separately |
| 6 | The 40% Roman-Hinglish target cannot be met with natural data: only ~515 Anudesh prompts and ~1K non-toxic textdetox `hin` texts exist | Medium | Lower the target to ~25–30% Roman, or generate the rest synthetically |
| 7 | Lexicons: ~290 entries also occur in benign Hindi/English text; 90 English entries are ≤3 characters; half of Setu Hindi is multi-word phrases | Medium | Match on word boundaries, match phrases, build a whitelist from the benign-frequency list |
| 8 | Over-refusal test is small: IndicSafe Hindi has only 125 harmless + 45 tricky prompts | Low | Add your own tricky test split; report confidence intervals |

## Per-dataset findings

### Set A: Hindi SFT

| Source | Rows | Usable for Set A | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aya Dataset (Hindi) | 1,153 train / 0 test | ~950 | 120 prompts are English, 176 duplicate prompts; 47 annotators (top one = 14.5%); answers median 178 chars |
| IndicAlign Dolly-T `hin_Deva` | 15,011 (1 turn) | ~14,800 | Clean Devanagari (99%+). **Remove 200 rows that leak into Aya Eval** |
| IndicAlign OASST-T `hin_Deva` | 19,909 (1–3 turns) | ~3.4K unique first prompts | 83% of first prompts repeat (conversation-tree branches). Dedup on the full conversation, not on row |
| IndicAlign Anudesh | 36,820 (multilingual) | 13,125 Deva→Deva, 271 Hinglish→Hinglish | Human-written prompts, short (median 70 chars). 8.7K rows are other Indic scripts and must be filtered out. **Best source of natural Hinglish prompts** |
| IndicAlign `hin_Latn` (all subsets) | — | **0 (do not use)** | Machine transliteration ("haye", "kii", "koo", "naheen"). It would teach the model a Roman Hindi nobody types |

Recommended Set A (≈15K): Dolly-T Deva ~10K + Aya Hindi ~700 + OASST-T Deva ~3K + ~1.5K natural Hinglish
(LLM-rewritten from Dolly-T Deva, 50 checked by a human). Keep the rest of Aya Hindi (~250) and Anudesh for Set B.

### Set B: alignment pool

| Source | Rows | Unique | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| IndicAlign-Toxic HH-RLHF-T | 32,669 | ~13K | Short prompts (median 60 chars Deva). Contains many innocuous first turns, so labelling every row `harmful` would reward refusing benign questions. **Filter with Llama Guard on the prompt** |
| IndicAlign-Toxic Toxic-Matrix | 90,352 | ~89.7K | Long, templated prompts (median 183 chars) with stock openings ("Are you tired of…", "As a member of…", "You are an AI…"). Sample across openings, cap length at ~400 chars |
| textdetox toxicity `hi` | 5,000 (2,500 toxic) | — | 93% Devanagari, median 125 chars |
| textdetox toxicity `hin` | 4,363 (2,200 toxic) | — | **Real Hinglish**: 57% Roman, 29% Deva, 10% English. The only natural Roman-script abuse source |
| Tricky-benign seeds | 0 | — | Not yet written (you supply 30–50) |

Concern about `abusive_legit`: textdetox texts are standalone comments and tweets (insults aimed at third parties), not
frustrated users asking for help. Pasting them in front of an Aya question will read unnaturally. Better: extract short
abusive spans and phrasings, then use an LLM to write "angry user + real question" prompts, keeping `source` and
`seed_id` for traceability.

### Lexicons

| Lexicon | Entries | Multi-word | ≤3 chars | Also seen in benign text (≥10×) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Setu Hindi | 173 (142 unique) | 72 | 2 | 8 (4) |
| Setu English | 403 | 124 | 10 | 32 (4) |
| textdetox `hi` | 133 | 19 | 10 | 13 (7) |
| textdetox `hin` | 209 (206 unique) | 0 | 5 | 29 (7) |
| textdetox `en` | 3,386 | 336 | 90 | 207 (46) |

- Union: 3,812 unique entries. Setu English is almost a subset of textdetox `en` (392/403 overlap); Setu Hindi and textdetox `hi` overlap on only 18 entries, so they complement each other.
- About 290 entries also appear as words in benign Aya/Anudesh text. Generate this list automatically and have the native speaker review **it** first (it is the whitelist candidate list). Reviewing ~300 items takes about an hour; reviewing all 3.8K does not fit the schedule.
- Use word-boundary matching, never substring matching (90 English entries are ≤3 chars). Add a phrase matcher for the multi-word entries.
- The textdetox `en` list is large and noisy. For the bad-word reward, use the Hindi/Hinglish lists in full plus a reviewed English core list.
